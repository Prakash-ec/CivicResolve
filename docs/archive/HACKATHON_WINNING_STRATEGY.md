## 2. Competitive Differentiation Points

1. **Not a Prototype:** Real server, real cryptographic JWT token verification, real database, and real AI inference.
2. **Community Validation vs. Solitary Reports:** Bridges citizen apathy by turning passive bystanders into active verifiers with the "Yes, This Issue Exists" hub and ResolveRewards™ incentives.
3. **Forensic Evidence Verification:** Prevents municipal fraud and spam by using multimodal AI to inspect photo metadata, scene consistency, and digital tampering.
4. **Geospatial Intelligence for City Leaders:** Transforms individual grievances into actionable spatial clusters and automated work orders rather than an overwhelming list of unconnected tickets.
5. **Universal Accessibility:** Trilingual voice dictation (Tamil, Hindi, English) empowers non-literate citizens to report issues with zero typing required.
